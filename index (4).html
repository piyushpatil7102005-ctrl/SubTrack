FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no plans or paywalls, and no card is needed to sign up.

Where is my data stored?

In your own browser's local storage, separately for each account. Nothing is uploaded to a server, so clearing your browser data also clears your subscriptions.

Will I see my data on another device?

Not yet. Accounts and subscriptions live in the browser where you created them, so another device or browser starts empty.

How do the reminder emails work?

While the app is open, it checks for subscriptions renewing within 3 days and emails you once per renewal date. Reminders can't be sent while the tab is closed, because there is no server running in the background.

What does "Add via bank message" do?

Paste a bank SMS or alert and the app extracts the merchant, amount, billing cycle, renewal date and autopay status. You review everything before saving, since parsing is best-effort.

What does autopay do here?

It doesn't charge anything. It only marks a subscription as auto-billed, so its renewal date rolls forward automatically once the date passes.

Why do I get a verification code?

It proves the email address is yours. You'll be asked for a code at sign-up, and again when you log in from a browser that hasn't been verified yet.
